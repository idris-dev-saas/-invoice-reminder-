# Invoice Reminder — Product Context

## Product Purpose
SaaS tool for freelancers and small businesses to manage invoices and send automatic payment reminders to overdue clients. Used daily, quietly, without drama.

## Users
Freelancers and small business owners who invoice clients regularly. Not tech-savvy. Stressed about money. Use the tool every morning for 5–10 minutes to check what's overdue and what's been paid. Need clarity, not excitement.

## Register
product

## Brand Tone
Calm, direct, professional. No celebration animations, no confetti. The product earns trust by being reliable and clear. Like a good accountant: precise, present, never flashy.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards (white + blue gradient header, big hero metric cards)
- "Fintech startup" aesthetics (navy + gold, aggressive CTAs)
- Over-animated onboarding flows
- Glassmorphism cards
- Any design that looks like it came from a free Figma template

## Strategic Principles
- Information density matters: show what's important without scrolling
- Color carries meaning (overdue = urgent, paid = resolved, unpaid = pending)
- Every interaction should feel instant and reliable
- Whitespace is not decoration, it's breathing room between tasks
